How It Works

A simple process designed around verification and customer communication.

1. Create an Account

Register with your name and email address, then follow any email-confirmation instructions.

2. Submit Your Gift Card

Provide the card information requested by the platform. Only submit cards you own or are authorized to trade.

3. Verification and Review

A submission must be reviewed before a payout can be approved. A submission is not a guarantee of payment.
